NOISE PATTERNS & INSIGHTS OBSERVED FROM MISSED MATCHES:
================================================================================
1. URL / Domain Names as Business Names:
   Source 3 frequently substitutes a company website/URL (e.g. 'xyzcorp.com') for the name.
2. Severe Typographic & OCR/Transliteration Errors:
   Letters transposed, phonetic spelling changes, or non-Latin script translations.
3. Missing / Blank Addresses in S2 & S3:
   When a matching S2/S3 record has no address AND its business name differs by more than legal suffix,
   name-only or address-only blocks fail to link them.
4. Street vs Landmark Address Discrepancies:
   In Indian records, street numbers are often replaced with landmarks (e.g., 'Near SBI Branch').
5. High Recall of Combined Strategy:
   The UNION of 4 deterministic strategies captures 88.16% of all GT pairs
   and 98.17% of entities, dramatically reducing the candidate space
   from 10.3 million to a manageable set per entity before downstream scoring.
